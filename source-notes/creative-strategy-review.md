# "Creative Strategy" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, Dara Denney, presenting her agency's research routine.
Sponsor: a stock footage service. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

It is a working research process, not a tactic. It is the strongest source for the
research and strategy skills.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Reputation walk.** Do what a buyer does before buying: the brand and its founders,
  best sellers and offers, the first page of search results, press, forums,
  marketplaces, social accounts, video reviews and their comments. The output is a
  short document of common objections and friction.
- **Review mining.** Export every review, add comments from the top ads of the last
  year, and ask: strengths and weaknesses, complaints, striking phrases, trends, what
  triggered the purchase.
- **Customer types, ranked.** Build them from the reviews and the reputation walk, and
  rank by how often and how strongly each shows up.
- **Before choosing formats.** Two or three angles per customer type; pillars, not
  one-off concepts.
- **Three gap checks.** Customer types in the reviews against those in the ad account;
  awareness levels (accounts lean too hard on ready-to-buy offers); variety across
  customer type, angle and awareness.
- **Planning rhythm.** A quarterly direction and a monthly plan, not weekly churn.
- **Offer is in scope.** A low price point may need a bundle before ads can work.
- Named examples: raising "is this a scam?" yourself; saying who the product is not
  for; adding an older customer type that the account ignored.

## What not to import as doctrine

Steps specific to online retail marketplaces. Each idea needs restating for services:
"reviews" becomes wherever that business's customers speak.

## Conflicts

She calls copying competitors, including sorting an ad library by impressions, the
worst strategy. The course and the second creator both model competitor ads.
