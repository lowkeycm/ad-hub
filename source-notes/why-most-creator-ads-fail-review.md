# "Why Most Creator Ads Fail" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, Dara Denney, September 2026. Sponsor: an influencer
marketing platform. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

It adds the step between research and production that other sources skip.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Diagnosis.** One or two sentences explaining why these ads, for whom. Without it
  there is testing but no strategy.
- **Who buys against who the ads address.** Build customer types from reviews, compare
  them with the people current ads speak to, and work the gap.
- **Lenses for the diagnosis.** Viewer awareness, customer types, format mix, three or
  four message pillars, and whether the winning message carries through to the landing
  page.
- **Five creator formats she reports scaling as of September 2026.** Authority, personal
  history, educational breakdown, investigation, transformation or comparison.

## What not to import as doctrine

- "30 to 50 percent of spend on partnership ads." The sponsor sells creator software.
- Stories presented as true that she says she does not verify.
- An AI-made "investigator" posing as independent. The structure is usable in the
  brand's own voice.

## Result

Feeds `ad-strategy` (diagnosis and gap checks) and the plays library.
