## Why it was reviewed

## Concepts worth retaining

- A person judges before anything ships. Output should be built so it can be judged
  quickly, with a stated reason it is good or bad.
- The ad, the offer and the landing page are one system. Creative cannot be optimised
  apart from the other two.
- Working alongside an agent in a workflow beats keeping a library of prompts.
