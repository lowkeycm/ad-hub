# "Meta ad basics" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, beginner walkthrough, presenter not named in the
transcript. Sponsor: an attribution tool. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

Supplied by the owner as part of the project context.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Image ad checklist.** High contrast, clean background, the change in the buyer's
  life over the specification, and an offer.
- **Video basics.** A person the viewer can relate to, bold text and captions for
  sound-off viewing, a vertical version and a square version, never a reused
  horizontal video.
- **Copy.** A 6 to 10 word claim or problem call-out, a three-step problem and solution
  shape, and a specific call to action in place of a vague "learn more".
- **Reading results.** Two numbers only: ROAS, or cost per conversion. Give a new ad
  days, not hours. One bad day is noise; a week of rising cost is a signal.
- Start with two or three variations of the first ad, each with one clear message.

## What not to import as doctrine

- "Advertising from the Instagram profile costs 30% more." No support is given.
- The sponsor's figures on how far platform tracking is off.

## Result

Adds little beyond the course. Three small plays are in the library.
