# "Static Ads Mastery" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, Dara Denney. Sponsor: an AI ad-generation tool, demonstrated
in the video. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

The most complete treatment of image ads among the sources.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Strategy before design.** One goal, one customer type, one level of awareness per
  image ad. Do not give one ad two jobs.
- **Hierarchy.** The headline is read first and does the targeting; then the product or
  key visual; then supporting elements.
- **One-second test.** Would a distracted viewer know what is sold and to whom at a
  glance? Clarity beats cleverness.
- **Copy principles.** Be specific; lead with a number; call out the audience; lean into
  what people avoid saying; make the desire the headline and the product the means;
  open a loop the landing page closes; name the fear; lift a striking phrase from a
  real review; show the transformation.
- **Format menu.** Infographic, headliner, benefit callouts, us against them,
  transformation, grid, text only. Three production levels: designed, polished photo,
  unproduced creator photo.
- **AI workflow.** A brand brief, research and gap analysis, three variants at a time,
  rounds of edits. She shows the failures: typos, too much text, cluttered layout. Not
  every output is usable.

## What not to import as doctrine

The sponsor's tool and its ad scores. The routine transfers; the tool is optional.

## Conflicts

One job per ad, against cover everything (second creator). Before and after images are
limited by platform policy in some categories.

## Result

Feeds `ad-creative` (static mode and its quality gate) and the plays library.
