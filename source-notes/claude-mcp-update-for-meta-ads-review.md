# "The Claude MCP Update For Meta Ads" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript from an agency owner (not named in the transcript; referred
to here as the second creator). Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

It walks through running an account audit, a competitor pull, creative generation and
a launch through connectors in one chat.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Account creative audit.** Classify every live ad by format, style and funnel stage,
  compare spend and results by class, and list the classes that are missing. The gaps
  become the production list.
- **A kill rule with numbers.** Cut an ad with no results once it has spent twice the
  account's average cost per result; cut an ad with one result once its cost is two and
  a half times the average.
- **The chain.** Audit, gaps, competitor examples, generate, launch as paused.

## What not to import as doctrine

- Copying top competitor ads as the plan.
- The AI video demo as proof. It was praised as identical to the product after it got
  the product name wrong. That is an argument for a review gate.
- The named third-party tools. Meta's connector now searches the Ad Library itself.

## Notes

- By chance alone, the first half of the kill rule cuts about one in seven ordinary
  ads and the second about one in five. Acceptable with plenty of new ads waiting, too
  aggressive without. Keep it as an option, with the multiples set per offer.
- He states that Meta orders ads within an ad set. The course says the same. Two
  sources now; treat it as a working assumption.

## Conflicts

Kill by spend multiple, against waiting for confidence on a schedule (course). Copying
competitors, against diagnosing gaps (Denney).

## Result

Feeds `ad-strategy` (audit mode), `ad-manage` (kill rule option), `ad-launch`.
