# "Meta Ad Hooks" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, Dara Denney. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from the transcript only;
her on-screen results could not be seen.

## Why it was reviewed

Nine specific hooks she reports working now, and a definition of what a hook is.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **A hook is three things at once.** What the viewer reads, what they hear, and what
  is in the first frame.
- **The nine hooks.** Trigger word placed in a proven hook; "why is it important?"; a
  day without the pain; three how-to forms (how to know if, how do I choose, how to
  start); "if you…"; "this one thing"; "I'm sorry" or "you're welcome"; a camera-filter
  look; a whispered voiceover. All nine are in the plays library.
- **How to test.** Three to six hooks per video. Take big swings; do not change one word
  to isolate a variable. Re-skin top performers with new hooks as one test.

## What not to import as doctrine

Nothing is discarded. The specific hooks will date, which is why each is a dated play
with a refresh, and why each business's own results decide what stays in use.

## Conflicts

Big swings between ads, against one type of change at a time at account level (course).
These fit together. Her how-to hooks meet the course's warning that teaching content
can attract industry peers.

## Result

Feeds `ad-creative` (hook spec) and the plays library.
