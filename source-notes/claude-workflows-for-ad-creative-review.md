# "Claude Workflows for Ad Creative" — evaluation notes

Source: YouTube transcript, Dara Denney. Sponsor: Anthropic. Reviewed 2026-10-03 from
the transcript only.

## Why it was reviewed

It shows how an agency uses an AI assistant with connectors for reporting and research.

## Concepts worth retaining

- **Weekly creative report.** Where the money went across ads, what rose over seven
  days, any new hit, and the specific lessons.
- **Hit rate.** The share of launched ads that become winners, tracked over time.
- **Competitor measures.** Ads launched in 90 days, average ad lifespan, share of
  genuinely new concepts, format mix, core messages, repeated phrases, customer types
  by share of ads, reliance on offers, and what is missing.
- **A known failure.** An assistant "analysing" an ad library is often not watching the
  videos or reading transcripts at all. Confirm what was actually seen.
- **Check the numbers twice.** Ask for a second pass that looks for errors in the first.
- Skills that encode how a strategist reads creative make the reports usable.

## What not to import as doctrine

The agency-management automations, and the praise for the sponsor's product.

## Result

Feeds `ad-review` (weekly report, hit rate) and `competitor-ads` (measures, and the
rule to say when a video was not seen).
